Southbag Online Banking is the primary account access surface for customers who have accepted that convenience and certainty are not always the same thing.

The service provides login, mandatory customer training, account access, balance viewing, money movement, lending actions, branch location, live support, recovery flows, security disclosures, and account closure. Each feature is presented as a formal banking workflow. The system may ask for more information than expected, may redirect you to training before access, and may periodically remind you that certain matters must be handled in person.

## First Access

On your first visit, Southbag requires completion of the Learn with Southbank training modules before the login portal remains available. This requirement is stored in your browser. If the record is missing, the service assumes your preparation is also missing and sends you back to training.

After training, return to the login portal and provide the requested identity, account, balance, code, number, and name details. Southbag accepts the submitted form and opens the account dashboard.

## Main Areas

- **Learning modules**: mandatory training before account access.
- **Login and recovery**: sign in, recover a password by code, or set up the secure portal password.
- **Account dashboard**: view balance, transfer funds, request loans, request free money, and manage unusually powerful account actions.
- **Support**: chat with a Southbag representative that can remember the conversation and exercise certain control actions.
- **Branch locator**: find an online or in-person branch, request directions, and wait for the map to finish deciding.
- **Security bounty**: review the official security issue inventory and reporting posture.
- **Privacy permissions**: manage browser-local consent, alert subscriptions, and permission prompts.

## Operating Expectation

Southbag treats the browser as part of the institution. Passwords, permissions, training status, portal setup, alert choices, and support history may be remembered locally. If you clear browser storage, Southbag may forget your obedience and ask you to repeat earlier ceremonies.

When uncertain, use one of the many support buttons. They all know where to send you.
